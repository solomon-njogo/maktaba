# Maktaba

Solomon Njogo's record of his physical books and where each one sits in his reading pipeline.

## Language

**Book**:
A physical volume Solomon tracks, whether it is on the shelf, Borrowed, or not yet acquired.
_Avoid_: Ebook, file

**Status**:
The pipeline state of a book: Reading, Done, TBR, or To-Buy.
_Avoid_: Progress, percent complete

**Reading**:
The status of a book that has been started and not finished.
_Avoid_: Resume, reading position, reading goal, bookmark

**Shelf**:
The books Solomon owns. A book is on the shelf when its status is not To-Buy. A borrowed book stays on the shelf.
_Avoid_: Book collection, holdings

**To-Buy**:
A book Solomon plans to acquire and does not yet own. It is not on the shelf.
_Avoid_: Wishlist

**Borrowed**:
A book that is out of Solomon's possession and still his. Borrowed is not a status.
_Avoid_: Loan, lent
